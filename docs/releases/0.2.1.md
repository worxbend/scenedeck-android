# SceneDeck 0.2.1

SceneDeck adds a full operator workflow and more ways to personalize your control surface.

## Added

- Workspace-specific named decks, local scene labels and preview freshness indicators.
- Optional Preflight checks and sustained live alerts, including deliberately armed microphone monitoring.
- Recording pause/resume and supported split/chapter tools, local timestamp bookmarks,
  and media progress/seeking in Mixer.
- Offline local History and JSON export with explicit gaps and unknown outcomes.
- Guarded multi-action presets with preparation, concrete output consent, cancellation
  and recorded results. Presets are invoked individually.
- Configurable foreground keyboard and compatible foot-pedal bindings.
- An isolated offline demo for practicing without changing real OBS connections or local data.
- Adaptive tablet Scenes and Mixer layouts, with retained state during resizing.
- Ocean, Rosewood, Amber and Paper palettes, bringing the fixed palette count to 15.
- Six accent choices and optional True black dark backgrounds, with preview, Apply,
  Cancel and Reset defaults. High Contrast and broadcast status colors preserve their roles.

## Reliability and compatibility

- Scoped persistence preserves existing curation; previous global curation remains available
  through explicit recovery. App organization and labels remain local to the device.
- Deferred actions retain their originating connection/collection. Timed-out writes are
  never retried automatically, and interrupted presets are never resumed automatically.
- Normal builds retain QR pairing. F-Droid source builds retain manual connections and
  the same operator/theming features without proprietary QR dependencies.
- Replay Buffer and Show rundown/cue scheduling remain excluded.

## Verification

- 1,169 local unit/render tests across 203 suites passed with no failures or skips.
- Full JDK21 quality/security gate, normal debug build/tests and F-Droid debug build passed.
- Emulator checks cover appearance preview/save/cancel, relaunch, rotation and demo isolation.
  Physical pedals/tablets, battery savings and measured device performance are not claimed.

Version code: 1012. Release APK/AAB artifacts are built and signed by the tag workflow.
