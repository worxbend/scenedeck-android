## Full description

**Stream from your PC. Control it from your phone.**

SceneDeck turns your Android phone or tablet into a beautiful remote control
surface for OBS Studio. Connect over OBS's built-in WebSocket server and run
your show from the couch, the stage, or the other side of the studio: switch
scenes with a tap, ride audio faders with live meters, start and stop your
stream or recording, and keep an eye on stream health in real time.

**Your deck, your scenes**

- Tappable scene cards with live screenshot previews — see what is on air
  without looking at your PC.
- Studio mode: drive preview and program separately, with transitions, a
  T-bar, CUT and automatic tracking of changes made from other OBS clients.
- Curate freely: assign roles, accents, icons and deck order. SceneDeck never
  touches your OBS scene collection — all curation lives locally on your
  device, with YAML/JSON export for backup.

**A mixer that moves with you**

- Full mixer page plus mini-strips on the deck, with live volume meters at
  50 ms refresh.
- Mute, faders, balance/pan, sync offset and monitoring type per source.
- Media controls: play, pause and restart media sources right from a strip.

**Stream & record with confidence**

- Start/stop stream, recording, virtual camera and replay buffer.
- Optional safety confirmations before anything goes live or stops.
- Elapsed time and status always visible.

**Know your stream health**

- Live stats dashboard: FPS, render time, dropped/skipped frames, CPU,
  bitrate and congestion, with trend charts.
- Connection Doctor and scene Graph views diagnose your setup before you go
  live.

**Built for Android**

- Home-screen widget: a mini-deck of your primary scenes, always one tap away.
- Ongoing quick-controls notification with live state while connected.
- Twelve theme families (including OBS, Dracula, Nord and Material You
  dynamic color), light and dark, with adjustable motion, density and haptics.
- Material 3 Expressive design, edge-to-edge, fully accessible.

**Requirements**

- OBS Studio 28 or newer (includes the obs-websocket v5 server built in).
- Phone and PC on the same network.

**Getting connected takes a minute**

1. In OBS Studio, open Tools → WebSocket Server Settings.
2. Enable the WebSocket server and note the port (default 4455) and password.
3. In SceneDeck, add a connection with your PC's IP address, the port and the
   password — that's it, your deck is live.

SceneDeck is a control surface, not a streaming tool: encoding, recording and
streaming all stay in OBS, exactly where you set them up.
